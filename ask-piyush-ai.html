The deployed FastAPI /chat endpoint was tested with 30 sequential questions across experience, skills, analytics projects, GenAI projects, portfolio and out-of-scope prompts. Requests were paced at five per minute, with three warm-up requests reported separately.

100%HTTP success · 30/30

Rate limits: zero HTTP 429 retries at the tested pacing. Warm-instance latency ranged from 1.39 s to 7.70 s.

Warm-up: 8.61 s, 6.15 s and 7.73 s across three requests. The first request is only a cold-start estimate if the service was idle; idle time was not recorded, so cold start is unverified.

Interpretation: experience questions were the slowest category (4.60 s mean, 4.00 s median). This run measures speed and availability only—not answer correctness, groundedness, refusal quality or concurrent-load performance.
